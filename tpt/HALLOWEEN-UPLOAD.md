## For each product (about 5 minutes each)
1. TPT, then Dashboard, then Add New Resource. Upload the **product PDF**.
2. Upload `...-PREVIEW.pdf` as the preview, and `thumb1` through `thumb4` as the 4 thumbnails, in order.
3. Paste the title, description and keywords from `listing.md`. Set the grades, subject and price.
4. In the description and tags, use the words **Ontario** and **Canadian**. Ontario teachers search for exactly that.

## After all 8 are live
- Create a **bundle**: "Ontario Halloween Bundle K-8" at about 25% off the total ($33.50, so list it at **$24.99**).
  TPT builds bundles from your live products, so no extra file is needed.
- Make one product **free** for the week (the Grade 1 one is a good choice). Free downloads bring followers, and followers
  get emailed when you post new products.
