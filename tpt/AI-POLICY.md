# North Star Resources: AI and authorship rule (binding for every session)

Set by the owner on 19 Nov 2026, after Teachers Pay Teachers announced (Oct 2026) that AI-generated resources are banned,
including product files, covers, thumbnails, previews and listing descriptions.

## The rule
From now on, every resource sold under North Star Resources is **created by the teacher (the owner)**.
Claude must not write, draw or generate the content of a resource for sale: no passages, questions, prompts, answer keys,
activities, artwork, covers, thumbnails or TPT listing descriptions.

## What Claude may still do (only if TPT's written policy allows AI-assisted work; check first)
- Look up and quote official Ontario curriculum expectations (Ministry text, see `curriculum/`).
- Find and cite sources so the teacher can check facts.
- Check the teacher's own drafts for factual errors or wrong answers, and say what is wrong; the teacher makes the fix.
- Business tasks outside the product: planning, scheduling, Facebook posts, reminders.

When TPT's written definition of "AI-generated" or "AI-assisted" is published, update this file to match it.

## Existing products
Everything in `products/` and `out/` (Halloween, Remembrance Day, Winter, Christmas, Jan-Feb, Curriculum Batches 1-2, the
kit art, thumbnails and listing copy) was generated with AI, except the owner's original Kindness Countdown PDFs in
`src/kindness/` (rebranded with AI-made covers and pages). Do not describe any of it as teacher-created.
Do not upload more of it to TPT. Live listings should follow TPT's written policy (the owner is contacting TPT Seller Support).
