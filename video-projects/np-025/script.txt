Nobody saw this coming.
Brighton are third in the Premier League. They beat Arsenal, they won at the Etihad, and they have the best goal difference in the country.
So, are Brighton for real? We went through the numbers. Four parts. The hype, the case for, the catch, and the verdict.
Part one. The hype.
After nine games, Arsenal lead on twenty four points. Manchester City have twenty one. And Brighton are third, on nineteen.
They have scored twenty four goals, more than anyone in the league.
They have conceded just seven. Their goal difference, plus seventeen, is the best in the Premier League by seven.
And they have kept six clean sheets, level with Arsenal for the most in the league.
Part two. The case for.
Arsenal have lost one league game all season. It was at Brighton, three nil.
Then on Saturday, Brighton went to the Etihad and beat Manchester City, one nil.
This is not a team that only beats the bottom half. On average, they take more shots than their opponents. Sixteen point six a game, against twelve point one.
And only Chelsea and Manchester City get more shots on target per game.
Part three. The catch.
Across the league, about one in three shots on target ends up in the net. Thirty two percent.
Brighton score with fifty two percent of theirs. The highest rate in the league.
At the other end, opponents score with just twenty one percent of their shots on target against Brighton.
If Brighton finished and defended like an average team, their goal difference would be about plus four. Not plus seventeen.
And fourteen of their twenty four goals came in just three games. Four nil against Villa, five nil at Coventry, and five nil against Palace.
Eleven different players have scored, and nobody has more than three.
Part four. The verdict.
Brighton are good. They have beaten the top two, and they control games.
But the finishing is running hot, and finishing like that usually cools off.
Our verdict? A top six team, for real. Title challengers? Not yet.
